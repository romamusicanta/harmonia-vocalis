## Who processes the data

The data controller is the Associazione Culturale Musicale Roma Musicanta, Via del Forte Tiburtino 98, 00159 Rome, Italy, info@romamusicanta.org.

## What data and why

The website does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools that track visitors.

The data you send with the contact and application forms (name, email, telephone if given, message) are used only to reply to you and, for applications, to arrange the audition. We do not share them with third parties and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

## YouTube videos

Videos load from the youtube-nocookie.com domain only when you press play. From that moment Google’s privacy policy applies.

## Members’ area

The members’ area is open only to the choir’s singers, with their own Google account. The singers’ data (attendance, section, contacts) are kept in the association’s records and are not published on the website.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data by writing to info@romamusicanta.org.
